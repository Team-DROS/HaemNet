Terms of use

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. What HaemNet is

HaemNet helps verified hospitals find willing blood donors. It is a communication tool. It is not a blood bank, a medical device, or a substitute for medical judgement.

2. Not medical advice or screening

HaemNet does not do medical cross-matching, donor screening or eligibility decisions. Hospitals and blood centres remain fully responsible for testing, screening and safe transfusion practice.

3. Hospitals

  • You must provide accurate registration details and documents. We may approve, reject or suspend an account.
  • Requests trigger real phone calls. Send a request only for a genuine need.
  • Keep staff sign-in details private and use donor information only to arrange a donation.

4. Donors

  • Donating is voluntary. Replying yes to a call is not a binding commitment, but please respond honestly.
  • Give accurate details and tell us if your availability changes.
  • Payment for blood is not permitted through HaemNet.

5. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the service, try to access other accounts, or send false requests. We may suspend accounts that do.

6. Availability and liability

We work to keep HaemNet available but cannot guarantee that a donor will be reached or will donate. To the extent the law allows, HaemNet is not liable for outcomes that depend on donor or hospital actions.
