Getting started

Four steps to your first dispatch.

  1. Register

    Enter your hospital's name, address, official email and the blood bank in-charge's phone number.

  2. Upload your documents

    Add your hospital or clinical establishment registration. Blood centres add their blood centre licence.

  3. We verify

    The HaemNet team checks your documents. You are told by email when you are approved.

  4. Dispatch

    Sign in and send your first request. Only verified hospitals can start calls to donors.

Verification protects donors. They are only ever called on behalf of real, registered facilities.

Hospital questions

Before you register

Who should register?

Hospitals, blood banks and blood centres that need to find voluntary donors quickly. Each facility registers separately.

Does HaemNet decide who is medically fit to donate?

No. HaemNet applies a 56-day cooldown and blood group search to build a call list. Your staff remain responsible for screening and cross-matching.

Do donors get called automatically?

Calls start only when a verified hospital submits a request. Your team chooses the blood group, units and urgency.

What does it cost?
