Delete your HaemNet account

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Donors

  1. Open the HaemNet donor app and go to Settings.
  2. Choose Delete profile and confirm.

This removes your donor profile and your sign-in credential from our servers and clears the app's local data. If you can no longer open the app, email haemnetai@gmail.com from an address you can prove is yours and include the mobile number you registered. We will confirm your identity before deleting.

Hospitals

Email haemnetai@gmail.com from the hospital's official email address with your hospital ID. We will remove the account, staff logins and uploaded licence documents.

What we keep

Records of past blood requests and calls (who was contacted and the outcome) are kept separately for audit and safety, and may be needed to meet legal obligations. Provider records held by our telephony and speech partners are governed by their own retention. If you want these reviewed, say so in your email and we will tell you what is kept and why.

Withdrawing consent

To stop automated calls without deleting your account, switch yourself to unavailable in the app or write to us. Read the privacy policy for your other rights.
