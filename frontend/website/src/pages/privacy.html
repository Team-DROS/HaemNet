Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what HaemNet collects, why, and your choices. HaemNet handles health related and location data, which we treat as sensitive, and we follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. Who we are

HaemNet connects verified hospitals with registered blood donors. For questions or requests, write to contact@haemnet.in.

2. Data we collect from donors

  • Phone number, used to sign in and to call or message you about matching requests
  • Name, blood group and preferred language
  • Registered location (one point, not continuous tracking), used to find donors within range of a hospital
  • Last donation date and donation history, used for the cooldown period
  • Your password, stored only as a salted hash

3. Data we collect from hospitals

Hospital name, address, official email, phone, registration identifiers, uploaded licence documents, contact person and staff sign-in details, plus the requests the hospital raises.

4. Calls, speech and transcripts

When a hospital sends a request, donors are called through our telephony provider. Call audio is processed by our speech and language provider (Sarvam AI) to understand your reply and speak to you in your language. The recent conversation is sent to that provider to interpret your answer. Transcripts are not written to our application logs. A spoken yes is followed by a separate confirmation question, and silence or unclear answers are not treated as consent.

5. What hospitals see

For a request that matches you, the hospital sees your name, blood group, your response and your approximate distance. Hospitals do not see your live location.

6. Why we use your data

To match requests to donors, place calls and messages you have agreed to receive, record responses and donations, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

7. Who processes data for us

Our hosting and database providers, our telephony provider (Twilio or a similar Indian provider), and Sarvam AI for speech and translation. They process data only to provide those services.

8. Consent and opting out

We ask for your consent when you sign up, including to receive automated calls about blood requests. You can withdraw consent or opt out of calls at any time by writing to us or using the app settings.

9. Retention and deletion

You can delete your donor profile in the app, or follow the steps on our account deletion page. This removes your profile and sign-in credential. Historical request and call records are kept separately for audit and safety and are not linked to marketing or other uses. Write to us if you want these records reviewed or erased where the law allows.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask to access your data, correct it, erase it, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. You can also complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

11. Children

HaemNet is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone under 18.

12. Security and breaches

Data is sent over HTTPS, access is authenticated, and secrets are kept out of code. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

13. Changes

We will update the date above when this policy changes and tell registered users about material changes.
