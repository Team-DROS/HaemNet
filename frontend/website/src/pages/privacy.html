Privacy policy

Last updated {{site.updated}}. This policy explains what {{site.team}} (“we”) collects through the HaemNet website, hospital dashboard and donor app, and why.

1. Who this applies to

HaemNet connects hospitals that need blood with registered donors. This policy covers donors, hospital staff and visitors to this website.

2. What we collect and why

InformationWhy we use it
Donor phone numberTo sign you in, match you to requests, and call or text you about a request.
Donor passwordTo sign you in. Only a salted hash is stored, never the password itself.
Donor name, blood group, preferred languageTo match you to requests and speak to you in your language.
Donor registered locationTo find donors within 10 km of a hospital. Hospitals see distance, not your live position.
Donation history (last donation date)To apply the 56-day cooldown and record fulfilled requests.
Your replies during a callTo understand whether you accept or decline. See section 4.
Hospital details and staff sign-inTo register, verify and secure hospital accounts.
Hospital verification documentsTo confirm a hospital is a real, registered facility before it can contact donors.
Messages you send through the contact formTo reply to you.

The donor app can ask for your location only when you choose to use GPS or confirm an area. It does not collect location continuously in the background. A profile photo, if you add one, stays on your device and is not uploaded.

3. Consent

We call and message donors only after they register and agree to be contacted about blood requests. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your profile or writing to us.

4. Voice calls and AI

  • Calls are made through a telephony provider. Your speech is processed in real time to understand your reply and to speak back to you.
  • We do not record or store call audio.
  • Transcribed text of your replies is sent to an AI provider to interpret your answer. Short fragments of transcribed text may appear briefly in technical logs while we operate the service.
  • A first “yes” is followed by a confirmation question. Silence, doubt or a technical error is never treated as acceptance.
  • We use pretrained models. We do not train models on your conversations.

5. Who else handles your data

We use the following service providers to run HaemNet. They process data only to provide their service.

We do not sell personal data. Data may be processed on servers outside India by these providers.

6. What hospitals can see

For a request that matches you, the hospital can see selected details such as your blood group, distance and response. Hospitals cannot see your live location.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your profile while your account is active. Dispatch and call records are kept so hospitals can audit past requests. When you delete your profile, we remove your profile and sign-in credentials. Some historical request records and provider records may remain for a limited period for audit and safety.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw consent. Write to {{site.email}} and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also complain to us about how we handle your data using the same address.

9. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Hospital accounts only see their own requests. No system is perfectly secure, so we also limit what we collect.

10. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add privacy-friendly analytics, it will not use cookies or follow you across sites.

11. Children

HaemNet is for adults who are legally able to donate blood. We do not knowingly collect data about children.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, where the change is important, tell you in the app or by email.
