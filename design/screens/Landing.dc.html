HOW IT HOLDS UP

Built for the worst fifteen minutes of someone's day.

Donor matching
A Neo4j graph holds donor relationships, geography and donation history, so blood group, radius and the 56-day cooldown resolve as a single traversal.
12 ms
Voice outreach
Sarvam AI speech models stream over Twilio, so the call sounds like a person in English, Hindi or Tamil. Calls go out concurrently, never one after another.
20 lines
Orchestration
A LangGraph state machine drives every call from initiate to routed, so a dropped line or a declined request never stalls the dispatch.

Accepted donors get push navigation to the exact ward. No app installed? An SMS with a tracking link goes out instead, so a willing donor is never lost to friction.
