About

Built to shorten the search for blood.

HaemNet is made by {{site.team}}, a team of three students who began with one question: why is finding a blood donor in an emergency still a round of phone calls?

Our mission

Give every hospital a fast, fair way to reach willing donors nearby, and give every donor a respectful way to say yes or no.

How it started

HaemNet began as a hackathon project. We kept building because the problem is real: manual calling is slow, and lists of donors go stale. The project has grown into a hospital dashboard, an Android donor app and a voice system that works in three Indian languages.

The technology, in plain words

  • Voice calls: placed through Twilio telephony.
  • Language understanding: Sarvam AI speech, translation and language models for English, Hindi and Tamil.
  • Matching: a MongoDB database with geospatial search to find donors by distance and eligibility.
  • Apps: a web dashboard for hospitals and an Android app for donors.

Our principles

  • Consent first. A donor's yes must be clear and confirmed.
  • Humans decide. Medical decisions belong to the hospital.
  • Honest limits. We say what HaemNet does and does not do.
  • Privacy by default. We collect what we need and explain why.
