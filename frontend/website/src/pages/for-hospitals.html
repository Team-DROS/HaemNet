For hospitals

Reach matching donors without phone trees and WhatsApp groups.

What you get

  • A command centre to raise requests by blood group, units and urgency
  • Live donor responses and a map of donor distance
  • Request history and donation logging
  • Works on desktop and mobile browsers

How to get started

  1. Register with your hospital name, address, official email and phone.
  2. Provide your registration details (for example ABDM Health Facility Registry ID and state Clinical Establishments registration) and upload supporting documents.
  3. Our team reviews your application and emails you when it is approved.
  4. Sign in and send your first request.

    5. Only approved hospitals can send requests, because every request triggers real phone calls to donors.
