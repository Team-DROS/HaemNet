About HaemNet

Finding the right donor quickly is still mostly phone calls. We are building the faster way.

HaemNet began as a hackathon project and is being developed into a production service for Indian hospitals. We use Indian language speech and translation models through hosted APIs so donors can talk to us in a language they are comfortable with.

Team

HaemNet is built by Team DROS, a group of computer science students.
