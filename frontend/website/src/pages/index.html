Find blood donors in minutes, not hours

HaemNet lets a verified hospital request a blood group and instantly reaches matching donors nearby with an automated call in their own language.

Register your hospitalDownload donor app

1. Hospital raises a request

Staff choose the blood group, units and urgency, then confirm before anything is sent.

2. Nearby donors are called

Donors within range who are likely to be eligible get a call in English, Hindi or Tamil.

3. Responses arrive live

Accepted and declined replies appear on the hospital dashboard as they happen.
