How it works for you

Three simple steps.

  1. Create your profile

    Sign up with your phone number and password. Add your name, blood group, preferred language and area.

  2. Get a call when needed

    If a nearby hospital needs your group and you are outside the 56-day cooldown, you may get a short call.

  3. Say yes or no

    You will be asked to confirm. If you accept, you receive directions to the hospital.

Your privacy

Your information, handled with care.

We collect only what is needed to match you to nearby requests and to call you.

Read the privacy policy

  • Nothing is recorded as a yes until you clearly confirm.
  • Hospitals see your blood group and distance for matched requests, not your live location.
  • You can sign out or ask for your profile to be deleted at any time.
  • The 56-day cooldown helps you avoid giving blood again too soon.
Donor questions

Before you sign up

Am I obliged to donate if I get a call?

No. You can say no, and a decline is simply recorded. Nothing is recorded as a yes without your clear confirmation.

Who decides if I can donate?

The hospital. HaemNet uses the 56-day cooldown and your blood group to decide who to call, but it is not a medical check. The hospital will assess you before donation.

Which languages can I be called in?

English, Hindi and Tamil.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete your profile in the app. You can also write to {{site.email}}. See the privacy policy for what is kept.
