1. What HaemNet is

HaemNet is a coordination tool. It helps verified hospitals contact registered donors who are nearby and appear eligible, and it shows the hospital how donors respond.

2. What HaemNet is not

  • HaemNet does not give medical advice, diagnose, or decide whether anyone may donate.
  • The 56-day cooldown and blood group search are conveniences, not clinical screening or cross-matching.
  • HaemNet is not an emergency service. In an emergency, contact your hospital or local emergency number.
  • HaemNet does not guarantee that any donor will answer, accept or arrive.

3. Hospitals

  • You must give accurate registration details and documents, and keep them up to date.
  • Only authorised staff may use your account. You are responsible for activity under it.
  • You remain responsible for all donor screening, testing, cross-matching and clinical decisions.
  • You must use donor information only to arrange a donation for a real patient need.
  • We may suspend or remove a hospital that gives false information or misuses the service.

4. Donors

  • Donation is voluntary. You may decline any request.
  • You must give accurate information, especially blood group and last donation date.
  • You are responsible for deciding whether you are fit to donate, and the hospital will assess you before donation.
  • HaemNet does not pay donors.

5. Acceptable use

Do not misuse HaemNet. That includes sending false requests, calling donors for non-medical reasons, attempting to access other accounts or data, scraping donor information, or disrupting the service.

6. Availability

We try to keep HaemNet available, but it may be interrupted for maintenance or because of problems with providers such as telephony or hosting. Do not rely on HaemNet as your only way to find blood.
