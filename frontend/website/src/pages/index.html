How it works

From request to confirmed donor in four steps.

  1. Hospital sends a request

    Choose blood group, units, urgency and location in the dashboard.

  2. HaemNet finds donors

    It looks for matching donors within 10 km who are outside the 56-day donation cooldown.

  3. AI calls everyone at once

    Donors are called in English, Hindi or Tamil and asked to confirm before a yes is recorded.

  4. You see every answer

    Responses appear live. Accepted donors get directions by SMS.

How donating works

You stay in control

  • Nothing is recorded as a yes until you confirm it.
  • Your exact location is not shared live with hospitals.
  • The 56-day cooldown protects you from repeat requests too soon after a donation.

The cooldown is an app rule and not a medical assessment. The hospital decides final eligibility.

Questions

Common questions

Does HaemNet replace the hospital blood bank?

No. HaemNet helps a hospital reach willing donors faster. Screening, cross-matching and every medical decision stay with the hospital.

How does HaemNet choose which donors to call?

It searches for donors with the requested blood group within 10 km of the hospital who are outside the 56-day cooldown. For emergencies you can choose all positive or all negative groups.

What if a donor says maybe, or does not answer?

Only a clear yes followed by a separate confirmation is recorded as acceptance. A maybe, silence or a failed call never counts as a yes.

Which languages are supported?

Voice calls work in English, Hindi and Tamil today.

What can hospitals see about donors?

Hospitals see selected details of donors matched to their request, such as blood group, distance and response. They do not see a donor's live location. Read the privacy policy for details.
