How it works

  1. Verified hospitals only

    A hospital registers with its official email and registration details. Our team reviews the documents before the account can send requests.

  2. Raise a request

    Choose blood group, units needed, urgency and location. You confirm before any call is placed.

  3. Donors nearby are matched

    HaemNet looks for registered donors within range with a compatible blood group who are outside their cooldown period.

  4. Automated, multilingual calls

    Donors get a call in English, Hindi or Tamil. The assistant asks if they can donate and then asks for a separate confirmation. Silence or unclear answers are never treated as a yes.

  5. Live dashboard

    Responses show on the hospital dashboard in real time, along with donor distance and request status.

  6. Donation recorded

    Once a donor donates, the hospital logs it and the donor's cooldown starts automatically.
